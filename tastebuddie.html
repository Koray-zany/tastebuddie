Send a WhatsApp message. TasteBuddie asks about your goals, allergies, health conditions, dietary preferences, your city, what ingredients you already have at home, and how many people you are cooking for.

FAQ

Got questions?
We've got answers.

How does TasteBuddie work? +
TasteBuddie runs entirely on WhatsApp. You send a message, answer a few quick questions about your goals and preferences, and TasteBuddie generates a personalised meal plan with full recipes — instantly.
Does TasteBuddie cover Nigerian food? +
Absolutely — Nigerian food is at the heart of TasteBuddie. From jollof rice and egusi soup to suya, zobo cocktails, and chin chin, we have a deep library of local dishes alongside international options.
Can TasteBuddie help with PCOS or diabetes? +
Yes. TasteBuddie has condition-specific plans for PCOS, diabetes, hypertension, and fibroids — with meals carefully selected to support your health while still being delicious.
Can I get a plan for my whole family? +
Yes. Tell TasteBuddie how many people you are cooking for and it adjusts portion sizes, ingredient quantities, and the shopping list accordingly.
Can I swap meals I don't like? +
Absolutely. Just tell TasteBuddie which meal you want to swap and it will suggest an alternative that matches your goals and preferences.
Is the free plan really free? +
Yes — the free plan gives you one daily meal plan per day with basic recipes at no cost. You can upgrade to Premium or Platinum for unlimited plans, weekly plans, condition-specific plans, and more.
Does TasteBuddie handle food allergies? +
Yes. Tell TasteBuddie your allergies once during setup — whether it is nuts, dairy, shellfish, gluten, or anything else — and every single plan it generates will automatically exclude those ingredients. You can also update your allergies anytime.
Does my location affect my meal plan? +
Yes! TasteBuddie uses your city to suggest ingredients that are easy to find in your local market. A user in Lagos will get different ingredient suggestions than someone in Abuja, Port Harcourt, or abroad.
Can TasteBuddie build a plan from what I already have at home? +
Absolutely. Just tell TasteBuddie what ingredients you currently have — rice, tomatoes, chicken, yam, whatever — and it will build a meal plan around what you already own, minimising waste and extra shopping.
